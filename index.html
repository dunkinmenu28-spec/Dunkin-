What Is the 7 Brew Secret Menu?

The 7 Brew secret menu is a set of unofficial drink combinations created by customers and baristas. They aren’t printed on the board. They’re built from flavors the stand already has, mixed in ways the regular menu doesn’t list. Think of it as a collection of recipes, not a hidden menu with its own prices.

Is the secret menu actually secret?

Not really. Spoon University put it well when it said the secret menu may not be a secret at all, since much of it is just customizations and flavor combos you can make yourself. Staff members have been known to point customers toward popular ones. At one store opening in Eau Claire, an employee encouraged people to look up secret-menu drinks shared online, including the Beyoncé Mocha, a blend of the Blondie and Brunette.

How does it work at the window?

Because these drinks aren’t in the register under their nicknames, the nickname may mean nothing to the person taking your order. Give the recipe instead: the base drink, the syrups and any toppings. The price follows the base drink, plus anything premium you add.

What are some popular secret menu drinks?

These recipes are fan-created, and they’re listed on this 7 Brew secret menu list along with others:

  • Raspberry Truffle: white chocolate and raspberry, usually on a macchiato or breve base.
  • Grasshopper: dark chocolate and mint, built on a mocha.
  • Lucky 7: a 7 Energy with a six-flavor mix.
  • Angel Food: vanilla, almond roca and caramel, usually on a breve or latte.

Does every stand have the same flavors?

No. Syrup availability can differ by location and by season, so a recipe that works in one town might need a swap in another. If a stand is out of something, ask the barista for the closest alternative.

Are there sugar-free secret menu drinks?

Often, yes. The 7 Energy and Fizz lines offer sugar-free options, so many fruit-based recipes can be adapted. Ask which flavors come sugar-free at your stand.
